I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](https://www.mpim-bonn.mpg.de), where my mentor is [Professor Daniel Huybrechts](https://www.math.uni-bonn.de/~huybrech/).

I received my PhD from Fudan University under the supervision of [Professor Jun Li](https://scms.fudan.edu.cn/info/2661/2694.htm). 

My research interests lie in algebraic geometry and related fields, with a focus on moduli spaces of sheaves and enumerative geometry.
